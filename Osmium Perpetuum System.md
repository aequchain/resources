SUBSYSTEM SPECIFICATIONS
OPS-R: RECOVERY SUBSYSTEM
Purpose

Extract and purify osmium from all end-of-life sources with ≥99.5% efficiency.
Recovery Pathways

Pathway 1: Electrochemical Recovery
For: Spent catalysts, concentrated sources

text

PROCESS SPECIFICATION

Principle: Electrolytic dissolution of PGM-containing materials
           followed by selective electrodeposition

Equipment:
- Electrochemical cell (Ti or Pt-coated electrodes)
- Acid-resistant vessel (PTFE-lined)
- Temperature control system
- Off-gas capture (for OsO₄)
- Power supply (renewable)

Electrolyte: 
- Primary: 2-4 M H₂SO₄ (sulfuric acid)
- Alternative: HCl/NaCl solution
- Additive: NaCl or NaBr (assists Os dissolution)

Conditions:
- Temperature: 40-80°C
- Current density: 10-50 mA/cm²
- Duration: 4-24 hours (depending on source)
- pH: <1 (acidic)

Chemistry:
Anodic dissolution:
  Os + 4H₂O → OsO₄ + 8H⁺ + 8e⁻
  (Os dissolves as osmium tetroxide)

OsO₄ capture:
  OsO₄(g) → OsO₄(aq) → captured in NaOH scrubber

Reduction:
  OsO₄ + 8H⁺ + 8e⁻ → Os + 4H₂O
  (Electrodeposition on cathode)

Yield: 99.5%
Energy: 40-60 kWh/g Os recovered (100% renewable)

Pathway 2: Distillation Recovery (Volatilization)
For: Mixed PGM sources, purification

text

PROCESS SPECIFICATION

Principle: Osmium forms volatile OsO₄ at relatively low 
           temperatures (boils at 130°C); no other PGM does this

Equipment:
- Oxidation furnace (sealed, temperature-controlled)
- Condenser system (cooled)
- Alkaline scrubber (NaOH solution)
- Safety containment (OsO₄ toxic)

Process:
1. Feed material placed in oxidizing atmosphere
2. Heat to 400-600°C with O₂ flow
3. Os oxidizes to OsO₄ (vapor)
4. OsO₄ condenses in cold trap (< 40°C)
5. Or absorbs in NaOH: OsO₄ + 2NaOH → Na₂[OsO₂(OH)₄]
6. Pure Os recovered by reduction (H₂ or electrochemical)

Chemistry:
  Os + 2O₂ → OsO₄ (at 400-600°C)
  OsO₄ + 4H₂ → Os + 4H₂O (reduction)

Yield: 99%+
Selectivity: Extremely high (OsO₄ volatility unique among PGMs)

SAFETY: OsO₄ is highly toxic
- Closed-loop systems mandatory
- Continuous monitoring
- Double containment
- Trained personnel only

Pathway 3: Bio-Enhanced Concentration
For: Low-grade sources, diffuse streams

text

PROCESS SPECIFICATION

Principle: Certain microorganisms bioaccumulate metals;
           use to concentrate Os from dilute solutions

Organisms:
- Primary: Metal-reducing bacteria (Shewanella, Geobacter)
- Secondary: Sulfate-reducing bacteria (Desulfovibrio)
- Alternative: Fungi (Aspergillus niger)

Mechanism:
- Biosorption: Metal binding to cell surface
- Bioaccumulation: Active uptake into cells
- Bioprecipitation: Microbial sulfide precipitation

Process:
1. Dilute Os-containing solution (<100 ppm)
2. Contact with microbial biomass in bioreactor
3. Microbes concentrate Os 100-1000×
4. Harvest biomass
5. Ash or electrochemically process biomass
6. Recover concentrated Os

Bioreactor Specifications:
- Type: Packed-bed or stirred tank
- Volume: 10-100 m³ per facility
- Retention time: 24-72 hours
- Temperature: 25-37°C
- pH: 6-8
- Nutrients: Minimal carbon/nitrogen source

Concentration Factor: 100-1000×
Yield from concentrated biomass: >95%
Energy: 5-10 kWh/g Os (low-grade recovery)
Advantage: Works on dilute sources uneconomical for chemical methods

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PRINCIPLE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Osmium forms volatile OsO₄ when heated in oxygen atmosphere:
  
  Os + 2O₂ → OsO₄ (g)    [at 400-600°C]

The volatile OsO₄ is captured by condensation or absorption in NaOH solution,

  OsO₄ + 4H₂ → Os + 4H₂O    [at 300-500°C]

    → Route to BIOLEACHING (concentration step)
    → Then to ELECTROCHEMICAL or VOLATILIZATION

IF material contains mixed PGMs:
    → Route to VOLATILIZATION first (separates Os from other PGMs)
    → Other PGMs to separate recovery lines

IF material is high-purity Os requiring upgrade:
    → Route directly to VOLATILIZATION (final purification)

   ├── Os recovery alone might not cover costs

3. SUBSTITUTION REDUCES DEMAND TO RECOVERABLE LEVEL
   ├── Current demand > recovery capacity (initially)
   ├── Post-substitution demand: ~500 kg/year
   └── Recoverable from existing circulation

4. INCENTIVE STRUCTURE ENSURES COLLECTION
   ├── High residual value motivates return
   ├── Tracking prevents leakage
   └── Regulations enforce compliance

5. NO ONGOING SUBSIDY REQUIRED
   ├── After initial capital investment
   ├── System generates surplus
   └── Surplus funds future development
